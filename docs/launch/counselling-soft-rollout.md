# Counselling soft rollout

This is a small, observable launch through relationships that already carry some context or trust. The aim is not to send every message at once. Begin with a few relevant groups and people, observe what happens, and let the response show where the next adjustment belongs.

Use the copy as a starting point. A sentence added in your own words about why you thought of a particular group or person will usually matter more than further polishing.

## Replies to common questions

### What does it cost?

A single 60-minute session is R850. The structured five-session process is R3,400. The free 15-minute fit call is only for establishing fit and asking initial questions, not a counselling session. If cost is a genuine barrier, you are welcome to mention it. A small number of reduced-rate spaces are kept where possible.

### What is the scope of the work?

It is supportive counselling and integrative wellbeing work. We can work with recurring patterns, anxiety and overthinking, relationship dynamics, transitions, emotional regulation, loss of direction, and the integration of contemplative experiences. It is not psychiatric diagnosis, medication management, emergency support, or a substitute for specialised clinical care where that is needed.

### Are sessions online?

Yes. Sessions take place online by video call. For this kind of reflective and relational work, the format tends to hold well, and many people find it easier to arrive settled from their own environment.

### Can someone work with you from outside South Africa?

Yes. The work is available online internationally. My professional registration is held in South Africa, and the service is offered as supportive counselling and wellbeing work rather than locally licensed clinical treatment in every country.

### Is this therapy?

It is not clinical psychotherapy and I do not present it as such. I am an ASCHP-registered Wellness Counsellor. The work is psychologically informed, relational, and practical. It can sit alongside therapy or clinical care when that is appropriate, but it does not replace those services where they are needed.

## Fourteen-day measurement plan

Begin with a limited number of messages on day 1. Add a second small set around day 4 or 5. Use the follow-up only where there is an existing relationship and the first message was appropriate. Review the pattern at day 7, then make one measured adjustment for the second week.

Do not judge a group only by immediate replies. Warm-referral journeys often include a quiet visit, a later return, or a private forwarding of the link. The useful question is whether the whole path produces enough movement to justify extending it.

### Day 7 review

Look at each source separately and ask:

* Did the message produce any visits?
* Did visitors reach the service that matched the message?
* Did anyone choose WhatsApp, a written enquiry, or a fit call?
* What questions appeared more than once?
* Where did people hesitate or misunderstand the offer?

Change one thing for week two. This might be the audience, the opening sentence, the page used, or the way price and scope are explained. Changing several things at once makes the result harder to read.

### Day 14 decisions

* If warm traffic produces enquiries and clients, continue careful referral distribution before adding paid advertising.
* If people visit but do not enquire, investigate page, offer, trust, pricing, or booking friction. Use the questions people actually asked as evidence.
* If messages generate little traffic, improve audience selection and outreach copy. A site redesign will not solve a message that is reaching the wrong people.
* Consider Instagram or Meta content when the limitation is reach, repeated familiarity, or the need for people to understand the work over time.
* Consider Google Ads only after checking real keyword demand, likely click costs, conversion rates, and client economics. Low search volume should be measured, not wished away.

The first aim is not scale. It is to find a small number of real paths through which the right people recognise the work, take a next step, and receive something genuinely useful.
