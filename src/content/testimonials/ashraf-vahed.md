It is a rare thing to encounter so much wisdom in one person. In my time with Michael, I have experienced a peace that only comes with deep trust: of being held without judgement, in a field of infinite compassion.

I have felt my best parts come forward, and a relatability to all sides of humanity, expressed with love and equanimity. It is rare for me to give so easily to the guidance of a man, and rare to find one with such a capacity to guide.
