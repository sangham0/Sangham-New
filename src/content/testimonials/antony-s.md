On meeting Michael for the first time, I was immediately drawn to his insightful, grounded approach. There is a depth to him that carries a sense of wisdom beyond his years.

What stood out most for me was his ability to create a space where men can simply be as they are. A space where they can show up without pressure or expectation. The way he holds space comes from a place of genuine love and care.

There is a natural quality in him that is rare, something that usually takes years to build in friendship, but was present from the moment we met.
