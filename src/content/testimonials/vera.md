Michael is a truly special person. Beyond the depth of wisdom and knowledge he shares, there is something deeply healing about his presence. During the workshop, I found myself softening, opening, and gently meeting parts of myself I had long found difficult to face.

He holds space with a rare combination of patience, authenticity, and humility, making it easy to feel safe and fully present with oneself. I left feeling more connected to myself, and deeply grateful for the experience.
