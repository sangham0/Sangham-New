My father, who lives with severe Parkinson's disease, joined one of Michael Kaplan's Sangham full moon meditation sessions. At the start of the evening, his tremors were particularly intense. He was visibly uncomfortable and struggling to sit still. About ten minutes into the meditation, however, he began to relax, and within another ten minutes he was remarkably calm.

My father regularly uses meditation to help manage his symptoms, but this experience felt very different. There was something powerful about the collective energy of the group, combined with the care and intention Michael brought through the full moon rituals and meditation practice.

I have also participated in group process work facilitated by Michael. He has a unique ability to guide people gently towards their edge of comfort, creating a space where meaningful insight can emerge while ensuring everyone feels safe, supported and grounded throughout the process.

Having practised yoga for over eleven years, I have experienced many different teachers and styles. Michael's sessions stand out as particularly profound. The integration of movement, breath, reflection and awareness of fellow practitioners creates a deeply thoughtful and connected experience. His teaching is both skilful and compassionate.

Sangham has always felt like a place where I could arrive exactly as I am. Michael has an extraordinary capacity to listen deeply, without judgement, and to offer wisdom that feels both practical and heartfelt. Conversations with him have often left me with a greater sense of clarity, perspective and connection.

Michael has created far more than a yoga and meditation studio. He has cultivated a genuine community centred on presence, care and personal growth. I am deeply grateful for the impact he has had on both my life and my father's.
